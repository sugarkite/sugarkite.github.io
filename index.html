A note in the box

Hi, we're Dani and Adrian from Sugarkite, a small studio in Bucharest. Mia & Pippa: Cozy Shop Sort is our first game.

The idea is Dani's: a puzzle you can play with a cup of tea, with shelves that slowly become tidy, shops that slowly become beautiful, and a clock that keeps things lively without ever being unkind. Adrian helped turn it into a real game on the technical side.

Pippa is the office cat. She has her own bed, a dozen ways to nap and very strong opinions about parcels.

The game is almost ready for Google Play. Before launch it needs testers on real Android phones. If you would like to play it early and tell us what you think, write to us; we read every message.

Dani & Adrian

Good to know

When can I play? The game is in its last tests before Google Play. Testers can join now by email; the public release follows.

Does it cost anything? No. It is free to play. Coins, boosters, unlimited lives and No Ads can be bought, and short videos you choose to watch give rewards. A short ad may appear between some levels, never during one.

Which phones? Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or newer.

Do I need an account? No. Your progress is saved on your phone.

How long is a level? Each level has its own clock, from under a minute to six minutes, and stars for finishing with time to spare.

Who is it for? Teens and grown-ups who like calm puzzles, tidy shelves and a bit of renovation.

Which languages? English, Romanian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Turkish, Russian and Traditional Chinese.

What about my data? The game's privacy policy will be published here before the game reaches Google Play. This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Something not working? Write to sugarkitegames@gmail.com; a real person answers.
